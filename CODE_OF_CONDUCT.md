# Code of conduct

Be useful and don't be a jerk.

- Argue about the code, not the person.
- No harassment, slurs, or "jokes" that are just that with a laugh track.
- Don't dump secrets, dumps of other people's machines, or exploit PoCs
  into issues.

Maintainers can refuse, edit, or ban. If someone is after you in this
project's spaces, email justin.slay@gmail.com.
